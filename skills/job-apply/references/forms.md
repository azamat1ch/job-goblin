# Forms, compensation and handoffs

Read the exact field and all available options. Profile owns contact facts, CV
owns evidence, answers.md owns reusable answers with date and jurisdiction.
A blank answer is unknown. Copy the answers actually used into the job packet.

## Form mechanics

Inspect the actual employer tenant, not just the ATS brand. Greenhouse, Lever
and Ashby can add extensive custom questions; Workday, SuccessFactors, iCIMS,
Oracle and Eightfold vary in account requirements. Resume autofill is an input
proposal: check every name, date, degree and employment entry it produces.

Check persisted dropdown selections, phone-country codes, uploaded filenames,
conditional questions and validation errors. Reinspect after navigation. Some
widgets show typed text without saving a structured selection. Some forms make
CV upload and profile import alternatives. Diagnose observed behavior instead
of blindly repeating clicks or copying selectors from another tenant.

Use the campaign's answers for relocation, availability, prior applications,
consents and employer contact. Ask only for missing answers that matter to this
form; don't infer them from nationality, employment or browser defaults.
For forced experience numbers calculate the relevant work for that question.

## Compensation

Setup establishes whether the user wants a fixed answer, a researched range,
an agent-chosen figure within bounds or a handoff. Apply that policy without
asking again each time. Receiving an offer and accepting one are separate
choices; optimize for the user's chosen goal rather than an inherited low ask.

Research the exact listing first, then employer/level comparables and recent
local salary guides. Record currency, monthly/annual period, base/total,
bonus/equity treatment and evidence date. Distinguish advertised range, your
estimate, negotiable target and actual floor. Current pay is a personal fact;
expected pay can be a researched proposal under the user's instructions.

If the form needs one number, follow the agreed decision rule and keep the
exact answer in `artifacts/<job-id>/answers.md`. Do not convert uncertain equity
into invented cash. Keep answers consistent within a process; explain changes
when scope or new information warrants them. No HK salary amount is a default.

## Written answers and handoff

Why this role: one real need, matching evidence and a credible reason for this
team. A gap answer can explain adjacent proof and learning without claiming the
missing experience. Use candidate-voice and the requested length.

For candidate submission give the exact form link, upload file, unusual field
labels and ready-to-use answers. For authorized agent execution complete the
form and record its actual confirmation. A status handoff is not another
permission gate. A timeout after submit calls for checking confirmation and
application history before retrying the submission.

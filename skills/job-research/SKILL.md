# Research and experiments

Start with the decision and what evidence would change it: source gaps, weak
conversion, compensation, ATS behavior, portfolio proof or a negotiation choice.
Use current primary or authoritative sources for changing external claims.
Distinguish platform documentation, recruiter opinion, measured research and
inference. Preserve sources, date, local relevance and uncertainty.

Compare findings to this candidate's funnel and goals. A global salary survey
may not describe their employer/level; an outreach anecdote is not a conversion
experiment. End with an actionable recommendation or the smallest useful test.

An experiment has a hypothesis, a meaningful change, a cohort/stopping point,
an outcome such as screens or replies, and a keep/change/stop decision. Use
`experiment add` and `experiment close` (read help) for accepted tests. Record
confounders, sample size and response latency before claiming improvement.
Avoid changing many variables at once when it makes the outcome uninterpretable.

The user's instructions determine pace and ambition. There is no required
weekly cadence, experiment quota or ban on a strategy they choose. Research
should remove uncertainty that blocks action, not become an endless substitute
for applications and conversations.

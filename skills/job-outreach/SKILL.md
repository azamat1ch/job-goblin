# Outreach

Read the exact role or target, candidate evidence and existing contact history.
Use [contact and message strategy](references/strategy.md) when choosing a
person or angle. Use `candidate-voice` for the actual words.

Follow the user's current request and standing authorization for recipients,
channels and execution. A draft request produces a draft; an authorized send
continues through sending without a fresh ceremonial approval. Missing account
access is a capability issue to resolve, not a reason to invent a sent message.

Save exact job-linked text under `artifacts/<job-id>/`. Use `outreach add`
with its help for contact/channel/address/message fields. After the event use
`outreach mark` for sent or replied. Keep follow-ups in the existing contact
record with `outreach follow-up`, exact text and the next due date when relevant.
A mailbox draft and the local artifact can coexist; neither proves a send.

For a named employer with no tracked role, keep a private named artifact and
report what happened; do not fabricate a job merely to satisfy the tracker.
Follow the user's chosen cadence and scope. Check replies before following up,
and honor their changes without importing a seven-day gap or contact quota.

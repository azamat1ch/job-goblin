# Find the person and make the case

Start with a named contact on the posting, then the team's public pages,
professional profiles, relevant talks/projects, or an internal recruiter for
the function. Return a useful short selection with current role, public URL,
relationship evidence and uncertainty. Senior title alone is not proof that
someone manages this vacancy. An agency contact may represent a different client.

Choose a person able to evaluate the proof or route the application. Use public
business contact information when available; label an inferred address as
unverified. Follow the user's requested contact scope rather than building an
unrelated people database. No fixed number of contacts is an intrinsic rule.

Build the message from the actual role: one problem, the strongest relevant
proof and an appropriate next step. An engineer may respond to a concrete
project question, a recruiter to role/availability clarity, a known contact to
a direct referral request. Do not claim friendship, referrals or familiarity
that the evidence does not establish. Research should improve relevance, not
stuff a message with personal trivia.

If the candidate has already applied, include the requisition/link when useful.
For a follow-up retain the thread's context, add useful information if available,
and make the next step easy. Learn from replies across a meaningful cohort;
one silence does not establish that a channel or message strategy failed.

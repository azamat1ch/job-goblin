# Candidate voice

Read the candidate-voice section in `config/private/preferences.md` and any
writing samples it references. The assistant's private voice file controls chat;
this skill controls words another person will receive as the candidate.

1. Identify recipient, channel, purpose and requested length. Follow the user's
   wording and register choices. If samples exist, extract their actual habits:
   sentence length, directness, vocabulary, warmth, punctuation and formality.
2. Write around one relevant need, supported proof and a useful next step.
   A scheduling reply needs exact dates and timezone, not another sales pitch.
   A cover letter may need a fuller case; a connection request may need no ask.
3. Read aloud against the samples. Remove generic praise and repeated claims.
   Preserve the user's edits instead of restoring your favorite phrasing.
4. Check names, numbers, titles, dates, links and relationship claims against
   evidence. Save reusable style corrections in the existing preferences section.

Without samples, propose a short plain professional draft and learn from its
correction while continuing the task. Suggested brevity is a starting point,
not a universal word cap. The user can request formal, playful, terse or detailed
writing. Do not transplant the original author's style or the goblin's grammar.

During setup use [voice calibration](references/calibration.md).

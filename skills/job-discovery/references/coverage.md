# Coverage audit

For each important role lane and market, sample relevant employers and an
independent search route outside the enabled sources. Compare useful live
postings against the database by employer, requisition and URL, checking aliases.

For each useful miss distinguish:

- missing source or unavailable account
- source query omitted an alias, sector or location
- pagination/date cap or failed parser
- normalization/deduplication mistake
- candidate-configured exclusion
- incomplete hydration or poor fit judgment

Report the sample size, queries/date, useful misses and smallest repair. A
small convenient sample cannot prove comprehensive market coverage. Repair the
actual bottleneck before adding more connectors. Repeat when the user's review
cadence or a material gap warrants it; no baked-in weekly schedule or target rate.
